The Knot Diary

The Knot Diary is a luxury wedding planning platform designed to help couples organize every part of their wedding journey in one seamless experience.

The platform simplifies complex planning tasks such as budget management, vendor booking, guest tracking, moodboard creation, and wedding timelines through an elegant and intuitive interface.

Designed for modern couples, The Knot Diary focuses on reducing wedding planning stress while creating a collaborative and emotionally engaging experience.

Role Product Designer

Project Type Mobile Application

Duration 6 Weeks

Tools Figma, Photoshop

Read Time 6 min

View Prototype View Design Process

01. Discovery (Research & Insights)

Wedding planning can quickly become overwhelming for couples due to scattered tools, budget stress, vendor coordination, and timeline management.

User research was conducted to understand how engaged couples currently organize their wedding planning process and identify common frustrations throughout the journey.

Research focused on:

  • Budget tracking challenges
  • Vendor communication
  • Guest management difficulties
  • Inspiration organization
  • Timeline coordination

Key Insight: Most couples rely on multiple disconnected platforms, creating confusion and unnecessary stress during planning.

02. Define (User Problems)

Based on the research findings, several major pain points were identified during the wedding planning journey.

  • Difficulty tracking wedding expenses
  • Managing multiple vendors across platforms
  • Lack of collaborative planning tools
  • Overwhelming timelines and task management
  • Scattered inspiration and moodboards

Problem Statement: Modern couples need a centralized and emotionally engaging wedding planning platform that simplifies organization while maintaining a beautiful and stress-free experience.

03. Ideate (Wireframes & Planning)

Multiple concepts and workflows were explored to simplify wedding planning while keeping the experience elegant and emotionally engaging.

Key Features Included:

  • Wedding dashboard with countdown
  • Budget planner and expense tracking
  • Guest RSVP management
  • Moodboard inspiration system
  • Checklist and timeline planning
  • Save the date management

The goal was to create a seamless and intuitive wedding planning experience for modern couples.

04. UI Design (Luxury Bridal Experience)

The visual design was inspired by luxury editorial aesthetics and modern bridal experiences.

A soft neutral palette featuring dusty mauve, ivory, beige, and sage green was used to create a calming and elegant interface.

The interface emphasizes:

  • Clean layouts
  • Soft visual hierarchy
  • Elegant typography
  • Minimal interactions
  • Spacious components

The overall goal was to create a wedding planning experience that feels premium, emotional, and stress-free.

05. Validate (Usability Testing)

Usability testing was conducted to evaluate how effectively users could navigate wedding planning tasks within the platform.

Participants were asked to:

  • Manage budgets
  • Explore vendors
  • Track guest RSVPs
  • Create moodboards
  • Complete wedding checklists

Key Findings:

  • Faster task organization
  • Improved planning clarity
  • Reduced cognitive overload
  • Better collaboration experience
  • Higher overall user satisfaction

Conclusion:
Testing confirmed that The Knot Diary successfully simplifies wedding planning while maintaining a visually engaging and emotionally supportive experience.
