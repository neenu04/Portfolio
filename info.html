Neenu Sara Saji is a UI/UX Designer focused on crafting intuitive and meaningful digital experiences.

She designs interfaces where clarity, emotion, and usability meet—ensuring every interaction has intent, not decoration.

With a background in Business, Information Technology, and Computer Science, she brings a structured yet creative approach to problem-solving and product thinking.

Her process is rooted in curiosity, research, and experimentation, focusing on turning complex problems into simple, human-centered experiences.

Beyond UI design, she is interested in interaction design and motion, exploring how micro-interactions make digital products feel more alive.

She also runs a small hamper business, "Curation_by_NeenuSara", and collaborates in photography and event volunteering, which strengthen her storytelling and visual sensitivity.

Outside of work, she enjoys exploring places, observing interactions, and experimenting with visual ideas that shape her growth as a designer.
